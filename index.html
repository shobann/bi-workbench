Two reports, two numbers: find where the definitions diverged

A finance director has two reports open on the same screen. Both claim to show revenue for October. The numbers differ by an amount that matters, and the meeting is in an hour. Someone asks which one is right, and the honest answer is that you don't know yet.

I've been on both sides of that question — defending a number, and being the person who quietly undermined one. What I've taken from it is that this is rarely a bug. A report doesn't produce a number. It produces a number plus a dozen decisions about what to include, most of them made by someone who has since moved to another team.
